# meld-realtime

words versus tone. a small local system that turns a stream of speech into a per-utterance interaction state (what the words say, what the voice says, whether they agree, a fused MELD emotion) and picks a short grounded reply. text + audio track, everything on cpu through onnxruntime, nothing remote.

setup, results and the real-time definition land here as the parts come in. design in docs/design.md, decisions in docs/decisions.md.
